MASS

MASS is a TON Jetton designed to reflect participation and interconnectedness within the Syntōpolis ecosystem.

Rather than representing financial value, MASS serves as a digital indicator of involvement in the social processes that shape the community. It reflects the relationships, interactions and collaborative activities through which participants contribute to the emergence and development of a new social system.

MASS is intended to make constructive participation visible and transparent, highlighting the importance of human cooperation and collective creation within Syntōpolis.

Purpose

Community participation and social coordination.

Technical Information

  • Blockchain: TON
  • Standard: Jetton
  • Name: MASS
  • Symbol: MASS
  • Official project: Syntōpolis
